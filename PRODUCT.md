# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are researchers and prospective research collaborators evaluating Minhyoung An's work, research fit, and publication record. Secondary users include faculty members, conference contacts, and technical recruiters.

## Product Purpose

Present Minhyoung An's research focus, publications, compact education context, awards, and contact paths in a form that can be scanned quickly and cited accurately. Success means a visitor can understand the research direction, inspect representative work, copy a BibTeX citation, and find a credible contact or identity link without searching through the page.

## Positioning

The profile is centered on real-world human activity and context understanding through on-device small language models, sensor data, privacy-aware inference, and energy-conscious systems.

## Operating Context

Visitors commonly arrive before or after reading a paper, meeting at a conference, considering collaboration, or evaluating research alignment. They scan education beside the identity, then move through research interests, publications, and awards.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript hosted with GitHub Pages.
- Japanese, English, and Korean interfaces with remembered language selection.
- Publication year filtering and expandable, copyable BibTeX records.
- DOI-backed title links where available.
- Responsive desktop and mobile layouts.
- Existing factual content must remain sourced from the repository; do not invent affiliations, metrics, citations, awards, or claims.

## Brand Commitments

- Use the name "Minhyoung An" consistently.
- Keep the tone academic, direct, multilingual, and credible.
- Preserve ACM Computing Classification System terminology for research interests.
- Use Apple Machine Learning Research, Google Research researcher profiles, and polished MIT/Stanford faculty homepages as craft-level references for a conventional academic profile; borrow their clarity and restraint, not their institutional branding.
- Avoid flashy AI-startup presentation and heavily editorial magazine-like composition.

## Evidence on Hand

- Research profile and publication data: `data.js`
- Localized biography and education: `i18n/ja.js`, `i18n/en.js`, `i18n/ko.js`
- DOI and BibTeX metadata for published work: `data.js`
- Awards: `data.js`
- Identity and contact links: ORCID, GitHub, and email in `data.js`
- No portrait photograph, citation metrics, laboratory logo, testimonials, or press assets are currently available; future design work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Keep education compact beside the identity; reserve full lists for publications and awards.
2. Make scholarly evidence easy to scan, open, and cite.
3. Treat multilingual clarity as a core capability, not an add-on.
4. Prefer factual precision and restraint over promotional claims.
5. Keep direct contact and researcher identity visible without competing with the work.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain keyboard navigation, visible focus, sufficient text contrast, reduced-motion support, reduced-transparency support, semantic headings, and responsive layouts without horizontal overflow.
